# HR Attrition Analysis
Exploratory data analysis of employee attrition to identify key factors driving turnover with PowerBI

### Executive Summary
** Overall attrition is 16.1%: 237 of 1,470 employees left, which is above the healthy range of roughly 10-15%. <br>
** Sales Representatives (39.8%) and Recruiters (37.5%) have the highest attrition, more than double the company average. <br>
** senior and technical-leadership roles are stable having attrition rate between 2.7-6.8%. <br>
*** Compensation, travel and overtime are major factor of attrition. <br>
