# HR Attrition Analysis
Exploratory data analysis of employee attrition to identify key factors driving turnover using Power BI(Power Query, DAX) and Business Acumen.

### Executive Summary
* Overall attrition is 16.1%: 237 of 1,470 employees left, which is above the healthy range of roughly 10-15%. <br>
* Sales Representatives (39.8%) and Recruiters (37.5%) have the highest attrition, more than double the company average. Senior and technical-leadership roles are stable having attrition rate between 2.7-6.8%. <br>
* Compensation, travel and overtime are major factor of attrition. <br>
* One in three employees in their first two years leaves (34.5% at year 1, 31.6% at year 0). <br>
